other financial instruments involves **substantial risk of loss** and is not

appropriate for you in light of your financial condition, investment experience,
and risk tolerance.

## Specific Risks

### 1. Market Risk
The value of securities and other financial instruments can fluctuate
significantly. You may lose some or all of your invested capital.

### 2. Options Risk
Options trading involves unique risks, including the potential for rapid and
total loss of the premium paid. Writing (selling) options may involve
potentially unlimited risk.

### 3. Cryptocurrency Risk
Cryptocurrencies are highly volatile and largely unregulated. Values can
change dramatically in short periods. Exchanges may be subject to hacking,
fraud, or insolvency.

### 4. Leverage Risk
Trading on margin or using leveraged instruments amplifies both gains and
losses. You may lose more than your initial investment.

### 5. Technology Risk
Software systems, including NAE, are subject to:
- Bugs, errors, and unexpected behavior
- Network outages and connectivity issues
- Data feed delays or inaccuracies
- Execution delays or failures

NAE does not guarantee uninterrupted or error-free operation.

### 6. Backtesting Limitations
Historical backtest results are simulations based on past data. They:
- Do **NOT** indicate or guarantee future performance
- Do **NOT** account for all real-world market conditions
- May be subject to look-ahead bias, survivorship bias, or data errors
- Do **NOT** include all transaction costs, slippage, or market impact

### 7. Algorithmic Analysis
Statistical analysis, heuristics, and pattern detection:
- Are **NOT** guarantees of accuracy
- May produce false positives or false negatives
- Should **NOT** be the sole basis for investment decisions
- Are provided for informational purposes only
